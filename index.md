So-connectAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to So-connectAI. So-connectAI is an AI-powered social media content creation and planning application currently under development.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use So-connectAI. We aim to handle personal information responsibly and transparently, in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), where applicable.

3. Information we may process

Depending on the features you use, So-connectAI may process:

* Social media post text, drafts and scheduled publication dates.
* Social media platform selections.
* Topics, audiences, objectives and tone preferences entered for AI content generation.
* Information you voluntarily include in prompts or post content.
* Technical information necessary to operate and troubleshoot the application, if collected by the hosting environment.

Please do not enter passwords, payment card details, sensitive personal information or confidential information into content prompts.

4. How information is used

Information is used to:

* Generate and help edit social media content.
* Save and manage drafts.
* Organise planned and scheduled posts.
* Provide search, filtering and post-management features.
* Maintain, test and improve the application.
* Respond to privacy or support enquiries.

5. AI content generation and third-party providers

So-connectAI currently supports AI content generation through two possible providers:

Ollama: If you select Ollama, prompts are sent to the Ollama service configured on the computer running the application. In the current local setup, this uses a locally running Ollama service.

OpenAI: If you select OpenAI, the prompt and information included in it are sent to OpenAI through its API to generate content. OpenAI processes that information under its applicable terms and privacy policies.

You should review the relevant provider’s privacy documentation before using its service:

* OpenAI Privacy Policy: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/
* Ollama: https://ollama.com/

So-connectAI does not control the independent privacy practices of these providers.

6. Storage and security

In the current development version, saved posts are stored in a local SQLite database on the computer running the application.

The developer API credentials are configured through environment variables rather than being intended for display in the application.

Reasonable security measures should be used to protect the computer, database, API credentials and any future account connections. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

If So-connectAI is later hosted online, its storage, access controls, backups and security arrangements may change. This policy will be updated to reflect those changes.

7. Social media integrations

At the date of this policy, official social media account connections, OAuth authorisation and automatic publishing integrations are still under development.

So-connectAI does not currently claim to have access to your LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest or Threads accounts.

If social media integrations are introduced, this policy will be updated to explain the information accessed, permissions requested, how access tokens are protected, and how users can disconnect their accounts.

8. Sharing of information

So-connectAI does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by an AI provider when you choose to use that provider for content generation. Information may also be processed by infrastructure or hosting providers if the application is deployed online in the future.

Any future sharing arrangements will be explained in an updated version of this policy.

9. Data retention and deletion

In the current local development version, saved posts remain in the application’s local database until they are deleted or the database is otherwise removed.

You can delete saved posts through the application’s post-management features.

Information sent to third-party AI providers may be subject to those providers’ own retention policies.

If online accounts or cloud storage are introduced, separate retention and deletion procedures will be explained here.
